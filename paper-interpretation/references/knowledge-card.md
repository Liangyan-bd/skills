# Knowledge Card

Use this reference when the user wants reusable notes, a reading archive, a wiki/Obsidian-style output, or future paper selection help.

Add a compact knowledge card near section 4 or as a final structured block.

| 卡片项 | 内容 |
|-|-|
| 可复用概念 | Concepts, variables, mechanisms, or taxonomy axes worth remembering. |
| 可复用方法 | Experimental protocol, measurement method, algorithm pattern, proof trick, or engineering workflow. |
| 可复用写法 | How the paper frames the problem, orders evidence, writes limitations, or separates claims from results. |
| 可追踪关键词 | Search terms, systems, datasets, benchmarks, authors, or related subfields to monitor. |
| 会修正的旧认知 | What prior belief this paper should update, weaken, or complicate. |
| 下次遇到类似论文先看什么 | Figures, tables, metrics, sections, or failure modes to inspect first. |

Rules:
- Keep the card compact. It is for future retrieval, not another summary.
- Use paper-specific nouns; avoid generic entries like "method", "experiment", or "future work".
- If the paper is not worth keeping as long-term knowledge, state that and only keep the 导读决策表.
